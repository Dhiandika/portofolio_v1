## The Era of Blandness

For years, we've stripped away everything that made the web feel "real". No shadows, no borders, just floating rectangles in a white void.

### Enter Neo-Brutalism

It's not about being ugly. It's about being **honest**.
-   **High Contrast**: Readability is king.
-   **Hard Shadows**: Objects should feel like they occupy space.
-   **Raw Colors**: ugly-pretty is the new pretty.

This portfolio is an experiment in that philosophy. We are rejecting the clean, sanitized corporate memphis style for something that feels like it was built by a human, not a committee.
