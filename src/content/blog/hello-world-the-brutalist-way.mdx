## Welcome to the Grid

This is the start of something new. We are stripping away the polish to reveal the structure underneath.

### Core Principles
1.  **Function over Form**: If it doesn't work, it doesn't matter how pretty it is.
2.  **Raw Materials**: Use standard web fonts, high-contrast colors, and solid borders.
3.  **Honesty**: Don't hide the grid. Celebrate it.
